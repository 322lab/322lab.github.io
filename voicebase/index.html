Six stages, one pass. Drop it after your tuning plugin and let two adaptive stages — De-Ess and Ride — do the fussy part automatically. Use it whole, or pull out just the one piece you need.

AUTO

De-Ess and Ride carry an AUTO badge. While it's lit, the knob is locked under automatic control. Turn AUTO off first if you want to dial it in manually.

BASE / FLAT

BASE and FLAT, at the top of the plugin, are one-click resets. BASE brings back the recommended starting point (both AUTOs on); FLAT strips everything back to a true, untouched pass-through. Neither one touches your Vocal/Narration choice or Flavor settings — so you can A/B processed vs. untouched in a single click.

Every knob, explained

What each control actually does.

Two panels, eleven controls. Here's what changes when you move each one.

Auto ModeVOCAL / NARRATION

Sitting top-left, above the whole plugin, this switches the tuning between sung and spoken material — shifting things like where De-Ess targets its band, whether AUTO is on or off.

Voice Repair

De-Ess0–100% · AUTO

A split-band de-esser that tames harsh "s" sounds (sibilance) without dulling the rest of the voice — the 2–4kHz body stays untouched. Centered at 5.5kHz in Vocal mode, 7.5kHz in Narration mode.

Gate−60 to −20dB

Cuts room noise and mic bleed below the threshold you set. Move it up toward −20dB to cut more; down toward −60dB to let quieter sound through.

Ride0–100% · AUTO

Auto-levels the take against its own running average, phrase to phrase, instead of a fixed loudness target.

Limit−3 to −0.3dB

Sets the output ceiling with a soft-clip curve, built to avoid the harsh crackle of hard clipping. Closer to −0.3dB is louder with less headroom.

In / Out±24dB

Input and output trim — gain-stage into the chain, then match your level back out.

Mix0–100%

Blends the fully processed signal back with the dry input.

Tone & Comp

Bass±10dB

Low shelf. Cut for a thinner, cleaner low end; boost for more body.

Treble±10dB

High shelf. Boost for air and presence; cut to tame a bright or sibilant source.

Comp0–100%

Compression amount. 0 (bottom) is no compression, MAX (top) is maximum, shown as a percentage. The actual character shifts with the Flavor setting (SMOOTH / BALANCED / PUNCH).

FlavorSMOOTH / BALANCED / PUNCH

Changes the character of Bass, Treble, and Comp — from gentle and transparent to more aggressive.

One plugin, two voices

Sung or spoken, it's still a voice.

Voice Base ships with two starting points tuned for how each one actually breaks.

Vocal

Great vocal plugins are built for the final mix — deep, powerful, and easy to lose three days in when all you needed was a quick demo for your bandmates. This is the plugin I wanted for that moment: drop it after your tuner, add whatever space you like underneath, done. (Honestly, I built it because I wanted it myself.)

  • De-Ess tuned for sung sibilance, 5.5kHz center
  • Ride matches phrase-to-phrase level, not word-to-word
  • Sits well after your pitch-correction plugin of choice

Narration

Voice Base does one job: get a voice to a clean, solid baseline — narration included. If the mic budget didn't stretch as far as the performance did and the recording came out a little soft or blurry, this is exactly the problem it's built to fix.

  • De-Ess tuned higher, 7.5kHz, for closer mic placement
  • Ride keeps delivery level across a whole take, not just a phrase
  • Turn AUTO on and it runs with little adjustment
Character, your way

For natural sources, or for something more produced.

Flavor gives you three characters for Bass, Treble, and Comp.

SMOOTH

For natural sources. Acoustic guitar, solo voice-and-guitar, anything where you want the performance to breathe. Wide and gentle — the compressor leaves the attack of each note untouched.

BALANCED

The standard. Start here.

PUNCH

For modern, produced sources. Tighter, denser, more upfront — good for pushing a podcast voice forward, or programmed tracks.

Mix and match freely — SMOOTH bass with a PUNCH comp works just fine.

What's actually in it

Built to be finished, not flashy.

No pitch correction, no reverb, no harmony generator. One job, done well — and built so you only ever use the parts you actually want.

ON/OFF

Use all of it. Or just the one thing you need.

Turn on both AUTOs and there's very little left to decide. Sibilance settles, levels even out phrase by phrase. You don't need a mixing background to get started — it works the moment you drop it in. Already have other plugins you like? Every one of the six stages has its own independent switch, so you can run just the De-Ess, or just the Ride, as a single-purpose effect — without replacing the rest of your chain.

Built not to break

01. Smoothed Parameters

Turning a knob mid-take doesn't zipper or click. Every stage is parameter-smoothed.

02. Word-Safe Gate

A 50ms hold keeps the gate from chopping off the tail of a word.

03. Verified Build

Passes pluginval and Apple's auval — the plugin loads and behaves the way a host expects it to.

BEYOND VOCALS

De-Ess and Ride are voice-tuned — turn them off and what's left is a plain Gate/EQ/Comp/Limiter channel strip. PUNCH suits drum and percussion buses; SMOOTH works well as a gentle glue on acoustic guitar or bass.
